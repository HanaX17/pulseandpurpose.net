TidyTidy Support

Help & frequently asked questions

Get help

  • Email hello@pulseandpurpose.net. We read every message and reply promptly.
  • Including your device model and iOS version helps us help you faster.

TidyTidy helps you sort and clean your camera roll, one quick decision at a time. File keepers into albums, find duplicates, and clear clutter privately on your iPhone. Here are the questions we hear most.

How do I organize my photos?

Open a photo set and swipe: keep it, file it into an album, bookmark it for later, or queue it to delete. You can create and rename albums in a tap, and changes show up right away.

Are my photos private? Does anything get uploaded?

Yes, private. TidyTidy runs entirely on your iPhone. Your photos and choices are never uploaded, sold, or shared, there are no accounts, and there are no ads. See our Privacy Policy for the details.

What happens when I delete a photo?

Deletions go to the iOS Recently Deleted album, where they stay recoverable for 30 days. The Photos app remains the source of truth, and TidyTidy always asks before deleting anything.

What do the plans include?

Free includes 150 organizing actions per day. TidyTidy Plus raises the daily limit to 300. TidyTidy Unlimited removes the limit entirely. Monthly and yearly options, with your local prices, appear in the app. An action is delete, keep, favorite, or add to album; bookmarking is always free and never counts.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. They auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open TidyTidy, then Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

In iOS Settings → Notifications → TidyTidy.

How do I change which photos TidyTidy can see?

In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → TidyTidy.

What does “Find duplicates” do?

It scans your library for near-identical shots and bursts so you can clear the extras and reclaim space. The first scan can take a minute on a large library; after that it is quick.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset review progress reopens every photo for review. Your all-time totals, and your actual photos, favorites, and albums in iOS Photos, are untouched.
