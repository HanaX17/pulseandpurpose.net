Help & frequently asked questions

PhotoToGo helps you swipe back through your photos, sort them into albums, clear the noise, and relive the cheerful moments, all privately on your iPhone. Here are the questions we hear most.

How do I organize my photos?

Open a photo set and swipe: keep it, file it into an album, bookmark it for later, or queue it to delete. You can create and rename albums in a tap, and changes show up right away.

Are my photos private? Does anything get uploaded?

Yes, private. PhotoToGo runs entirely on your iPhone. Your photos and choices are never uploaded, sold, or shared, there are no accounts, and there are no ads. See our Privacy Policy for the details.

What happens when I delete a photo?

Deletions go to the iOS Recently Deleted album, where they stay recoverable for 30 days. The Photos app remains the source of truth, and PhotoToGo always asks before deleting anything.

What do the plans include?

Free every day: 150 swipes. PhotoToGo Plus raises the daily limit to 300 ($0.99/month). PhotoToGo Unlimited removes the daily limit entirely ($1.99/month). A “swipe” is one organizing action (delete, keep, favorite, or add to album); bookmarking is always free and never counts.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. They auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open PhotoToGo, then Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

In iOS Settings → Notifications → PhotoToGo.

How do I change which photos PhotoToGo can see?

In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoToGo.

What does “Find duplicates” do?

It scans your library for near-identical shots and bursts so you can clear the extras and reclaim space. The first scan can take a minute on a large library; after that it is quick.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset review progress reopens every photo for review. Your all-time totals, and your actual photos, favorites, and albums in iOS Photos, are untouched.
