Free to start · iPhone · iOS 17+ · No accounts, no ads

Sorting your photos, made almost fun.

Thousands of photos, no time to sort them. PhotoToGo turns the chore into a few minutes of swiping — and ends on a good memory, not a storage warning.

Swipe to sort

One thumb, one pass.

Swipe to keep, file into an album, bookmark, or delete. Your camera roll goes from chaos to sorted without feeling like work — and you can remap each swipe to fit your hand.

Relive the moments

The happy part of tidying.

PhotoToGo resurfaces the cheerful shots hiding in your library — this month, on this day in past years, the ones you forgot. Tidying up becomes a reason to smile.

Everything it does, and nothing it doesn’t.

Powerful where it helps, quiet everywhere else.

Clear the clutter

Find near-identical shots and bursts, clear the extras, and reclaim the space. Deletions go to iOS Recently Deleted, recoverable for 30 days.

Albums in a tap

Create and rename albums as you go. Every change lands in iOS Photos right away — never a second place your photos have to live.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, nothing uploaded. Your photos stay on your iPhone, and PhotoToGo always asks before deleting anything.

Ready to tidy up?

Start free with 150 swipes a day. Raise or remove the limit anytime — bookmarks are always free.
