NeatRoll Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 2, 2026

In plain English

  • NeatRoll runs entirely on your iPhone.
  • Your photos never leave your device through NeatRoll.
  • No accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party trackers.
  • We don’t run servers, so there’s nothing for us to collect.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple.

This Privacy Policy describes how NeatRoll (“the App”) handles information. NeatRoll is published by Pulse and Purpose, a registered DBA of APRIL’S CHOICE LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy is specific to the NeatRoll iOS app; our general website privacy practices are described in our site Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

NeatRoll runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your photos, usage data, or personal data. There are no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or ad trackers. Your photo library never leaves your iPhone through NeatRoll.

2. What the App accesses

Your photo library (with your permission). When you grant Photos access, NeatRoll reads your photos and their thumbnails through Apple’s PhotoKit so you can review, favorite, organize into albums, and delete them. The iOS Photos app remains the source of truth: deletions go to Recently Deleted (recoverable for 30 days), and favorites are mirrored as Apple Favorites. You can change or revoke this access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → NeatRoll.

3. What the App stores

All of the following is stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage), never on our servers (we don’t operate any):

  • Your review decisions (which photos you kept, favorited, organized, queued for deletion, or bookmarked);
  • Your settings (appearance, language preference) and your lifetime activity totals.

You can clear this at any time with Settings → Reset review progress, or by deleting the App.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not run servers of our own, and NeatRoll does not send us analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, ad networks, analytics SDKs, or cross-app trackers, and we never build a personal profile of you.
  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email, contacts, or location. (Photo metadata such as a capture date or place shown in the App is read on-device. Resolving a place name to display it uses Apple’s location services and is never sent to us or to any third-party tracker.)
  • We do not sell or share any data with anyone.

5. No analytics or tracking

NeatRoll does not collect analytics or usage data. It does not send app launches, organizing actions, photo counts, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers to us or to an analytics provider.

6. Subscriptions and Apple

NeatRoll offers optional auto-renewing Plus and Unlimited subscriptions with monthly and yearly billing options. All payments are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your payment details. Subscription status is verified on your device via Apple’s StoreKit. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Optional feedback

If you choose to email us at hello@pulseandpurpose.net, we will receive the message you send and your email address, and we use them only to respond to you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

8. Children’s privacy

NeatRoll is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

9. Your controls

Because everything is on-device, you are in full control: manage Photos access in iOS Settings, manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, and remove all app data by deleting the App.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@pulseandpurpose.net and we’ll respond promptly.
