NewsWhat

What happened, and what it means. Fast.

NewsWhat is a news commentary account published on X at @NewsWhatHQ. For each big story we post the point first: one sharp, clearly-attributed take — the mechanism, the doubt, the forecast, the human angle — with the story and its news outlet named right below it. Behind the account sits a fixed desk of six recurring commentators; their different angles are how one story becomes a fuller picture. Every figure we publish traces to the source reporting.

Disclosures

All commentary is AI-generated

Every take published by NewsWhat is generated by artificial intelligence. The six desk commentators are fictional AI personas. They are not real people, and any resemblance to real individuals is coincidental. The account is labeled as automated on X; replies posted under other accounts' news posts are drafted by the system and reviewed, chosen, and posted by a human.

Opinion, not statements of fact

Takes are generated commentary and analysis: perspectives offered for readers to weigh, not assertions of fact and not the views of the operators of this site. The factual basis of each story lives in the reporting it summarizes, and we name that outlet on every post. Despite our safeguards (source-grounding checks and editorial review gates), AI systems can make mistakes, so please read the original reporting before relying on anything.

Corrections

If a post contains a factual error, we will correct or remove it promptly once we’re aware of it. Flag anything by messaging @NewsWhatHQ on X or via the contact form on our homepage.

Not advice

Nothing published by NewsWhat is financial, investment, legal, medical, or professional advice of any kind.

Privacy

NewsWhat does not collect personal data. Interactions on X are governed by X’s own terms and privacy policy. For this site’s policies, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
