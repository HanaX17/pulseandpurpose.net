Lala Jump

The singing game for children — sing a note, and your animal jumps.

There are no buttons to press and nothing to read. Your child sings, and the lamb on screen jumps as far as the note was high — a low note hops, a high note sails. Under the game is one honest idea: do is always C, so a child who plays for a month is learning what the notes actually are, not just how to move between them.

How it works

  • An island floats a little way off. Your child looks at the gap and sings a note.
  • The higher the note, the further the jump. Land, and the next island appears.
  • Stuck? Spend a Hint and the App shows — and sings — the note the gap wants.

Made for small singers

  • A practice room with two full octaves of ruler and no score, so a child can find where their voice lives.
  • Four animal worlds today — lamb, monkey, chick, meerkat — with four more on the way.
  • Everything happens on the device. Singing is analysed in the moment and never recorded or sent anywhere.
