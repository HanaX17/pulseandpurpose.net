Lala Jump

Your voice. Your next jump.

Lala Jump is a little game powered by your voice. Sing and hold a note to send your animal across floating islands — higher notes make longer jumps. Stick the landing, then see how far you can go. Under the game is one simple idea: do is always C, so the notes you get to know here are the real ones.

How it works

  • An island floats a little way off. Look at the gap and sing a note.
  • The higher the note, the further the jump. Land, and the next island appears.
  • Stuck? Spend a Hint to see the note the gap wants, and tap to hear it.

What’s inside

  • Four animals — lamb, monkey, chick, and meerkat — each with its own world and its own personal best.
  • A Practice room with two octaves of ruler and no score: tap a note to hear it, watch your animal jump, then sing it yourself.
  • Easy or Hard, Low notes or High notes — play in the range that suits your voice.
  • Share your result card with a friend.
  • Everything happens on your iPhone. Your singing is analysed in the moment and never recorded or sent anywhere. No ads, no tracking.

Get it

Lala Jump is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
