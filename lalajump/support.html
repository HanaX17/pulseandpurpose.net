Lala Jump Support

Help & frequently asked questions

Need help?

  • Email hello@pulseandpurpose.net. We read every message and reply promptly.

How do you play?

Sing one note. The higher the note, the further the animal jumps. Land on the next island and keep going. That’s the whole game — most children work it out in a few seconds by trying it.

The animal doesn’t jump when we sing

Three things fix almost every case: make sure microphone access is allowed (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Lala Jump), try a quieter room — background noise can drown a small voice — and sing one steady note rather than a word or a phrase. Holding the note for about a second is what makes the jump happen.

Which singing range should we pick?

High notes (C4–C5) fits most children’s voices. Low notes (C3–C4) fits most adult voices, so a parent can play alongside. Tap either card to hear its starting note — pick the one that feels comfortable to match.

What does a Hint do?

A Hint shows the note the current gap is asking for — the ruler appears with the target marked, and a button plays the note out loud. Each run starts with three, and you earn another every ten islands.

What is a second chance?

From island 20 onward you earn a second chance every twenty islands. If a jump misses while you hold one, it is spent automatically and the animal goes back to try that island again, keeping the run alive.

What is the difference between Easy and Hard?

Easy gives a wider landing zone — visibly wider islands — and asks only for the seven natural notes at first. Hard asks for more precision and includes the half-steps (like do♯) from the start.

How does Game Center work?

Game Center is optional and off by default. A parent can sign in from the App’s settings (the quiet ☰ button on the home screen); after that, runs go to Apple’s leaderboard and the player’s Game Center name appears on the result card. Signing out in iOS Settings turns it all off again.

Is our singing recorded?

No. Audio is analysed on the device, in the moment, to find the pitch — and that’s all. Nothing is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

How do we start the record book over?

Open settings from the home screen and choose Clear records. This clears best scores and recent runs on the device. Scores already on a Game Center leaderboard are managed by Apple and stay there.

When are the other animals coming?

Four worlds are open today — the lamb, the monkey, the chick, and the meerkat. The penguin, duckling, panda, and rabbit are finished and on their way in a future update.
