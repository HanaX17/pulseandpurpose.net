Lala Jump Privacy Policy

Last updated · September 5, 2026

In plain English

  • Lala Jump listens to singing on your iPhone only. Audio is analysed in real time and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
  • We don’t run servers, so there’s nothing for us to collect.
  • Game Center is optional, off by default, and run by Apple.

This Privacy Policy describes how Lala Jump (“the App”) handles information. Lala Jump is published by Pulse and Purpose, a registered DBA of APRIL’S CHOICE LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy is specific to the Lala Jump iOS app; our general website privacy practices are described in our site Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

Lala Jump is a singing game for children: sing a note, and the animal on screen jumps as far as the note was high. Everything the App does with your child’s voice happens on the device, in the moment. We do not collect, record, upload, sell, or share audio, usage data, or personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers.

2. The microphone

With your permission, the App listens through the microphone only while a game or the Practice screen is open. The audio is analysed on the device in real time to estimate the pitch of the note being sung — that pitch number is the whole game. The audio itself is never recorded, never stored, and never leaves the device. There is no path in the App by which sound can be saved or transmitted. You can revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Lala Jump; the App will keep working, but the animal will have nothing to listen to.

3. What the App stores on the device

All of the following stays only on the device, never on our servers (we don’t operate any):

  • Game settings — the chosen singing range, difficulty, and world;
  • The record book — recent runs and best island counts;
  • If Game Center is signed in, the player’s Game Center display name, so the result card can show it.

Settings → Clear records removes the record book, and deleting the App removes everything.

4. Game Center (optional, run by Apple)

Lala Jump can connect to Apple’s Game Center so runs appear on a leaderboard. This is off until an adult signs in from the App’s settings; the App never shows a sign-in sheet on its own. When signed in, Apple provides the player’s Game Center nickname and photo (shown on the result card), and the App submits each run’s island count to Apple’s leaderboard. That data is processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Game Center terms — it does not come to us. Signing out of Game Center in iOS Settings stops all of it.

5. Sharing a result

The share button turns the end-of-run card into a picture on the device and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded by the App; where that picture goes is entirely up to the person sharing it.

6. Children’s privacy

Lala Jump is made for children, and it is built so that there is nothing to collect: no audio recording, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no free-text input anywhere in the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. The one optional online feature — Game Center — is operated by Apple, is off by default, and is designed to be enabled by a parent.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not run servers of our own, and the App sends us nothing.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, ad networks, analytics SDKs, or cross-app trackers.
  • We do not require an account or collect names, emails, contacts, or location.
  • We do not sell or share any data with anyone.

8. Purchases

Lala Jump is currently free, with no purchases inside the App. If paid content is added in the future, payment would be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and this policy will be updated first.

9. Optional feedback

If you choose to email us at hello@pulseandpurpose.net, we will receive the message you send and your email address, and we use them only to respond to you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
