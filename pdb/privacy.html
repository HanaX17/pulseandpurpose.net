PDB Privacy Policy

Effective · June 2026

In plain English

  • You can use PDB without an account; an account adds comments and sync.
  • We collect the minimum needed to run the app and personalize your feed.
  • The “takes” are AI-written pastiche for discussion and entertainment — not real quotes, and they can be wrong.
  • We do not sell your data or use third-party ad trackers.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple.

This Privacy Policy describes how PDB (Personal Daily Brief) (“the App”) handles information. PDB is published by Pulse and Purpose, a registered DBA of APRIL’S CHOICE LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Unlike our on-device apps, PDB is a server-backed service, so this policy explains the data that flows through it.

1. The short version

PDB turns the day’s news into short summaries and multi-voice AI commentary, with a personalized feed and an optional community. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app, we do not sell your personal data, and we do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app ad trackers.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. You can use PDB without an account. If you create one, we store your email address and an encrypted password (or an Apple sign-in identifier), plus a display username if you set one.
  • Your preferences. Interests, content languages, saved and liked stories, muted sources, appearance, and notification settings — so we can personalize your feed.
  • Usage & interactions. Which stories you open, read, like/dislike, save, share, or skip, and approximate reading time — used to rank your feed and improve the product. A device identifier (Apple IDFV) and basic device/OS info are recorded for analytics and, if you enable them, push notifications.
  • Content you submit. Public comments you post (shown with your username) and questions you ask the in-app panel.
  • Push token. If you turn on notifications, we store the Apple push token for your device so we can deliver them. Turning notifications off deletes it.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, health, or financial data.

3. How we use it

  • Personalize and rank your feed and recommendations.
  • Operate core features (accounts, saved items, comments, the panel chat).
  • Send notifications you have opted into (you can turn these off any time).
  • Measure and improve performance, reliability, and content quality.
  • Keep the community safe (moderation, abuse prevention) and meet legal obligations.

4. AI-generated content

The “takes” in PDB are AI-generated commentary written in the styles of well-known voices — clearly-labeled stylistic pastiche for discussion and entertainment. They are not real quotes, are not affiliated with or endorsed by those people, may contain mistakes, and do not represent the views of PDB or its makers. Nothing in the app is professional, financial, legal, or medical advice.

5. How we share it

We share data only with service providers that run the app on our behalf, under contract: Supabase (database, authentication, hosting), OpenRouter (the AI provider that generates commentary — it receives news article text, not your personal data), and Apple (App Store distribution and push delivery). Public comments and your username are visible to other users by design. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Data retention

News articles and their commentary are short-lived (typically a few days) and are then hidden and later purged. Your account, preferences, and the content you posted are kept until you delete them or your account.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account and associated data from inside the app (You → Account → Delete account), or by emailing us.
  • Edit interests, mute or block sources, and turn notifications on/off at any time.
  • Request a copy of, or correction to, your data by contacting us.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA/UK, California), you may have additional rights to access, port, restrict, or object; we honor valid requests.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted by row-level security, and passwords are stored only in hashed form. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children’s privacy

PDB is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
