PDB · Personal Daily Brief

The world’s news, read and unpacked for you.

PDB is a calm daily news app. An advanced AI reads the world’s news for you, distills the stories that matter, and adds short, clearly-labeled takes worth talking about. Two tabs, two jobs: catch up fast, then go deeper.

See how it works Help & support

Two ways to read your day.

PDB opens to two tabs. They do different jobs, so you’re never made to choose between “what’s big right now” and “what’s interesting to me.” You get both.

Today

The quick scan.

Today is for catching up fast. It gathers the day’s biggest stories across every topic — world, business, tech, sport, and the rest — newest first, so a couple of minutes leaves you genuinely up to speed. It keeps going as you scroll: the headlines first, then the rest of the day as it happened, with or without commentary.

For You

The deeper dig.

For You is for discovery. It learns what you care about and reaches wider, surfacing the interesting, useful stories you wouldn’t have thought to look for. The more you read, like, and save, the sharper it gets — while still leaving room for the unexpected, so your world keeps widening instead of narrowing.

The news, and the conversation around it.

Every story comes with more than the facts.

Takes worth talking about

Each story carries short commentary in a range of distinct voices — clearly-labeled AI pastiche, written to spark a real opinion, never to tell you what to think.

Voice of the People

Read what other people are saying, add your own, and like the takes that land. The most-liked reader comment rises to the top of each story.

Always in your control

Tune your interests, switch content languages, mute a source, reset your For You feed, or report anything. The takes are the opinions of their personas — they can be wrong, and they are never the views of PDB.
