Personal Daily Brief

Need a hand?

Email hello@pulseandpurpose.net — we read every message and reply promptly. Your feedback directly shapes what we build next.

What are the “takes”?

They are AI-generated commentary written in the styles of well-known voices — clearly-labeled pastiche for discussion and entertainment. They are not real quotes, can be wrong, and do not represent the views of PDB or its makers. Always check the linked original source.

How do I personalize my feed?

Open the You tab to set your interests and content languages. PDB also learns from what you read, like, and save. You can reset your For You feed any time from the refresh button at the top-right of For You, or in Settings → History.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Notifications are managed in your device’s system Settings → Notifications → PDB.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel them in your Apple account: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to You → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and the data associated with it. You can also email us to request deletion.

Report a comment or content concern

Use the report option in the app, or email hello@pulseandpurpose.net. We review reports and remove content that violates our terms.
