1. Who we are

Pulse and Purpose is a technology company that builds AI-powered software and meticulously crafted physical products. Our legal entity is APRIL’S CHOICE LLC, doing business as “Pulse and Purpose.” When this Policy refers to “we,” “us,” or “our,” it refers to APRIL’S CHOICE LLC operating under the Pulse and Purpose brand.

For any privacy-related inquiries, you can reach us at hello@pulseandpurpose.net.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account information: When you sign up for an account, we may collect your name, email address, and a password.
  • Communications: When you contact us (for example, by email), we collect the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to share.
  • Content and AI inputs: When you use our AI-powered features, we collect the prompts, questions, files, images, audio, or other inputs you submit (“AI Inputs”), as well as the outputs returned to you (“AI Outputs”).
  • Payment information: If you purchase a paid product or subscription, our payment processor will collect your billing details. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the Services, such as pages visited, features used, time spent, and clickstream data.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, language preferences, and timestamps.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: See Section 6 below for details.

Information from third parties

If you sign in using a third-party identity provider (for example, Google or Apple), we may receive your name and email address from that provider in accordance with the permissions you authorize.

3. How we use your information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Services, including our AI-powered features.
  • Generate AI Outputs in response to your AI Inputs.
  • Respond to your requests, questions, and support inquiries.
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, and other harmful activity.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
  • With your consent, send you product updates, newsletters, or marketing communications — you can opt out at any time.

We do not sell your personal information. We also do not share your personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

4. AI-specific practices

Because our Services use artificial intelligence, we want to be transparent about how AI affects your data:

  • Third-party AI providers. We use third-party large language model (LLM) and AI infrastructure providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and similar) to process AI Inputs and generate AI Outputs. When you submit AI Inputs, those inputs may be transmitted to and processed by these providers under their respective privacy and data-processing terms.
  • Model training. We do not use your AI Inputs or AI Outputs to train publicly available foundation models without your explicit consent. Where our third-party providers offer data-handling commitments (for example, “zero data retention” or “no training on customer data” options), we configure our integrations to use those options where reasonably available.
  • Output accuracy. AI Outputs are generated probabilistically and may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or out of date. Do not rely on AI Outputs as a substitute for professional advice (legal, medical, financial, etc.).
  • Sensitive information. Please do not submit highly sensitive information (such as government identification numbers, health records, or financial account numbers) as AI Inputs unless you have a specific need to do so and have read the relevant product documentation.

5. How we share your information

We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers. With vendors who help us operate the Services — hosting, analytics, AI infrastructure, customer support, and payment processing — under contracts that require them to protect your information.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Pulse and Purpose, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business.
  • With your consent. Any other sharing only with your explicit consent.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your preferences, understand how the Services are used, and — where you consent — measure marketing performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect Site functionality.

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Services and for the purposes described in this Policy, then we delete or anonymize it. Specific retention periods depend on the type of data and applicable legal requirements. AI Inputs and AI Outputs are retained only as needed to deliver the requested feature, support you, and comply with our legal obligations.

8. Data security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@pulseandpurpose.net. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
