Moto Rampage Privacy Policy

Updated · October 20, 2026

The short version

  • The single-player career runs offline. No Moto Rampage account is required.
  • We do not collect personal information through the game or operate a game server. The game has no ads, in-app purchases, developer analytics, or advertising trackers.
  • Progress and preferences stay in local app storage. Optional Apple Game Center handles the online leaderboard under Apple’s policies.
  • If the iPhone is already signed in to Game Center, the game can connect silently at launch and sync automatically.

This policy describes information handled by Moto Rampage (“the App”), published by Pulse and Purpose, a registered DBA of APRIL’S CHOICE LLC (“we” or “us”). It applies to the iPhone game. Our website is covered separately by the website Privacy Policy.

1. Local game data

The App stores the following on your iPhone:

  • Your coin balance, lifetime race earnings, race progress and results, and settlement records used to avoid duplicate rewards.
  • Owned motorcycles, paint colors, engine upgrades, and your selected motorcycle and route.
  • Language, music, sound effects, tilt-steering preferences, and whether the introductory sequence has been shown.
  • If Game Center is used: Apple’s game-specific player identifier, per-account earnings and submitted-score records, and earnings waiting to sync. These help keep different Game Center accounts separate.

This local data is not sent to a developer-operated server. Optional tilt steering uses motion-sensor input on the device to steer; the motion readings are not saved or uploaded.

2. Apple Game Center is optional

The career works without Game Center. If you are already signed in to Game Center on the iPhone, the App can authenticate silently at launch and sync automatically. Otherwise, opening the leaderboard may present Apple’s sign-in. The App does not receive your Apple Account password.

When connected, the App reads your existing leaderboard score from Apple and submits your lifetime race-earnings score. Earnings recorded offline can be retried later. Spending game coins does not reduce that leaderboard score. Apple’s GameKit interface displays leaderboard names, rankings, and profile information.

Apple operates this service. Your nickname, score, and other Game Center information can be visible according to Apple’s service and your settings. Apple’s handling is described in Game Center & Privacy and Apple’s Privacy Policy. This is separate from the developer’s app-data practices.

3. Your choices, deletion, and backups

To stop Game Center syncing, sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings. Previously submitted leaderboard information remains managed by Apple; deleting the App does not automatically remove it from Apple’s service.

Deleting the App removes its local save. Device backup and restore settings may include and restore local app data. Moto Rampage has no developer cloud-save service. Game Center can supply a previous leaderboard score, but it does not restore coins, motorcycles, purchases, or career progress.

You can turn tilt steering off in the game and use the joystick instead.

4. No developer analytics or advertising

The App contains no advertising network, advertising-identifier tracking, developer analytics, or in-app purchase system. We do not receive app-usage events or device identifiers through a developer analytics service. Apple’s operation of Game Center and any system services you use is governed by Apple.

5. Support email

If you choose to email hello@pulseandpurpose.net, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as an issue description or screenshot. We use that information to respond and help resolve your request. Do not include passwords or Apple Account credentials. Support email is separate from data handled locally by the game.

6. This website

Visiting this support or policy website is separate from using the game. Website hosting and any site-wide practices are described in the website Privacy Policy. A statement about no analytics in the App does not mean website hosts or Apple services process no information.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when these app practices change.
