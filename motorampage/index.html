Moto Rampage / 暴走摩托

Moto Rampage

Arcade motorcycle racing and close combat for iPhone.

Race the pack. Trade blows.

  • Five routes: coast, midnight city, desert, forest, and snowy mountains.
  • Punch, kick, and steal a rival’s club. Watch for rivals who can take it back.
  • Finish races to earn coins for four motorcycles, paint colors, and engine upgrades.
  • Version 1.0 has no ads and no in-app purchases. The single-player career works offline.

Made for your thumbs

Use the joystick to steer, accelerate, and brake, and the strike button for close combat. Optional tilt steering is available. On iPhone Duo, the game adapts to the outer and unfolded screens; book mode adds a live race overview, while tabletop mode separates the race and controls.

Your next race

The coast is always free to enter. Later routes use coins earned in races for entry fees. The two fastest motorcycles also require the top-three finish shown in the Garage. Coins cannot be purchased or redeemed for money.

Help and privacy

Get help with controls, sound, progress, and the optional Apple Game Center leaderboard. Read the Privacy Policy for how local saves and Apple’s service work.

For iPhone. Requires iOS 17 or later. Available in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
