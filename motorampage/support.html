Moto Rampage Support

Updated · October 20, 2026

Need help?

  • Email hello@pulseandpurpose.net. Include “Moto Rampage” in the subject.
  • Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, and what happened. A screenshot is optional. Do not send passwords or Apple Account credentials.

How do I start and control a race?

Choose Coast Run on the main menu, then start from its route details. The starting motorcycle and coast route are available immediately. Push the left joystick up to accelerate, down to brake, and sideways to steer. The right Strike button attacks a nearby rival; move alongside before striking. A club becomes available through combat, and a rival can steal it back.

Why do some races or motorcycles cost coins?

Finish races to earn coins. Coast Run is always free; routes 2–5 charge an entry fee each time you start a race. The Garage uses earned coins for motorcycles, paint, and engine parts. The two fastest motorcycles also require the top-three finish shown in the Garage. Version 1.0 has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases; coins cannot be bought with real money.

How does tilt steering work?

Turn on Tilt Steering in Settings or the pause menu. Hold the iPhone comfortably when starting or resuming so it can calibrate. Turn tilt off to return to the joystick. Tabletop mode on iPhone Duo temporarily uses the joystick without changing your saved tilt preference. Rotating or folding during a race pauses it; resume when ready.

Why is the music quiet or missing?

Check the separate music and sound-effects switches in Settings or the pause menu, then check the iPhone’s media volume. Audio can play while the phone is in Silent mode. While another app plays non-mixable audio, such as music, the game’s background music stays quiet and returns when that audio stops; effects can still play.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The single-player career needs no internet connection or game account. Apple Game Center is optional and only the leaderboard needs its service. Declining sign-in or being offline does not prevent racing.

How does the leaderboard work?

Tap the coins / leaderboard area at the upper right of the main menu to open Apple’s Game Center interface. If this iPhone is already signed in to Game Center, the game can connect silently at launch and sync automatically. If it is not, opening the leaderboard can present Apple’s sign-in.

The leaderboard ranks lifetime race earnings, not your current wallet balance, so spending coins does not reduce your score. Offline earnings can sync later. Apple runs the leaderboard and manages its visibility and account settings. To stop syncing, sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Where is my progress saved?

Coins, motorcycles, purchases, race progress, and preferences are saved locally on this iPhone. Moto Rampage does not provide its own cloud-save or cross-device career restore. Device backup settings may include local app data. Deleting the app removes its local save; restoring a device backup may restore that data.

Game Center is a leaderboard, not a career backup. A score already stored with Apple cannot restore your wallet, motorcycles, or route progress, and switching Game Center accounts does not merge all earlier local earnings.

Something is not working

Send a description to hello@pulseandpurpose.net, including the steps that lead to the issue and any message shown. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep the local save. For privacy details, see the Privacy Policy.
