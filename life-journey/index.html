A place for life’s stories

Story App is a working name for an app that helps you record your own and your family’s stories through words, original audio, photos, and a family relationship map. This page provides the legal and support information for the current trial version.

The product name is not final. “Story App” and “故事 App（名称待定）” refer to the same product. The /life-journey/ website address is its current legal and support location.

Read before you start

  • Privacy Policy — local and cloud storage, phone sign-in, speech processing, and who can see a story.
  • Terms of Use — your content, permission to record other people, sharing, and account responsibilities.
  • Support — sign-in help, exports, privacy requests, and the current account-deletion process.
