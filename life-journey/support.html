Contact us: hello@pulseandpurpose.net

Please include “Story App,” your device and operating-system version, the App version if available, and what happened. You do not need to send private stories to begin a support request. Never send passwords or SMS verification codes.

Which version am I using?

Story App / 故事 App（名称待定） is a working name. The trial can run in local mode or in cloud mode. Local mode keeps the story library on this device and simulates family/friend interactions. Cloud mode uses a signed-in account and a shared backend. If you are unsure which mode is active, tell support what the account screen shows without sending a code.

I cannot receive or use a sign-in code

Check the country calling code and phone number, that your phone can receive SMS, and that your connection is working. Complete the security check before requesting a code. Follow any wait message before requesting another one and use the latest code. If the problem continues, contact us with your country/region and the error shown; do not include the verification code.

Recording or transcription is not working

Check the microphone and speech-recognition permissions in your device or browser settings. Web recording needs a secure connection or a local development address. Speech recognition depends on the operating system, browser, language, and network. Where recognition is unavailable, the App may record audio without a transcript; you can also type. Keep the recording screen open and confirm your recording was saved.

Where are my stories stored?

Local-mode content stays in the App’s device/browser storage; speech recognition may still use a provider’s server. Cloud mode uploads saved content, including original audio and photos, to Supabase. “Only me” controls the audience, not the storage location. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I export my records?

Open the profile / “Me” screen and choose the export-all option, including original audio and photos. Keep the resulting ZIP somewhere safe. Local mode supports restoring a compatible backup; cloud mode currently does not support restoring from a backup file. A backup may contain information about other people. Export before clearing local storage.

Who can read a story?

Check the story’s “Who can see” setting: Only me, Family, Family and friends, or Public. Drafts are author-only. Family-map information is shared separately with the family’s members. Public stories can be opened without signing in where public links are enabled. Review the entire story and obtain permission before sharing; outside copies cannot be recalled.

How do I request account or data deletion?

The current App does not have an in-app account-deletion button. Email hello@pulseandpurpose.net with the subject “Story App account deletion,” identify the account (for example, its phone number with country code), and state whether you want the account and associated data deleted or have a narrower request.

We will verify that the request is authorized and explain its scope before acting. Do not send a password or SMS code. This is a manual process; we do not promise immediate deletion or a fixed completion time. We will explain any required retention or shared information needing separate review. Signing out, leaving a family, or clearing a device does not itself delete a cloud account. Copies already exported or saved by others cannot be recalled. For local-only content, export what you need and remove it through the App or the device/browser’s storage controls.

Someone added or shared information about me without permission

Email us with the relevant story link or enough detail to identify it and a brief description of the concern. Avoid sending more personal information than needed. You can contact us even if you do not have an account. The same address handles copyright and misuse reports.

简体中文

联系我们：hello@pulseandpurpose.net

请注明“故事 App”，并说明设备、系统版本、App 版本（如可查）和遇到的问题。开始求助时无需发送私人故事。请勿发送密码或短信验证码。

我使用的是哪个版本？

Story App / 故事 App（名称待定）是占位名称。试用版可以运行在本机模式或云端模式。本机模式把故事库留在当前设备，家人和好友互动为模拟；云端模式使用已登录账号和共享后端。若不确定当前模式，可向支持人员描述账号页面所显示的内容，但不要发送验证码。

收不到或无法使用登录验证码

请检查国家或地区区号、手机号、短信接收能力和网络连接。申请验证码前先完成安全验证；如页面提示等待，请等待后再重试，并使用最新验证码。如仍有问题，请告知所在国家或地区及页面错误提示，不要附上验证码。

无法录音或转写

请检查设备或浏览器设置中的麦克风和语音识别权限。网页版录音需要安全连接或本机开发地址。语音识别能力取决于系统、浏览器、语言和网络；识别不可用时，App 可能仅保存原声而没有转写，你也可以打字记录。录制期间请保持页面打开，并确认录音已保存。

故事保存在哪里？

本机模式把内容存放在 App 的设备或浏览器存储中，但语音识别仍可能经过服务商服务器。云端模式会把保存的内容（包括原声与照片）上传至 Supabase。“仅自己”控制受众，不控制存储地点。详情见隐私政策。

如何导出记录？

在个人资料或“我”页面选择导出全部（含原声和照片），将生成的 ZIP 文件妥善保存。本机模式支持恢复兼容备份；云端模式目前不支持从备份文件恢复。备份可能包含他人的信息，清除本机存储前请先导出需要保留的内容。

谁能看见故事？

请查看故事的“谁能看”：仅自己、家人、家人和好友、公开。草稿仅作者可见；家庭关系图独立向家庭成员共享。在启用公开链接的版本中，公开故事无需登录即可打开。分享前应检查完整内容并取得授权，已经流出 App 的副本无法撤回。

如何申请删除账号或数据？

当前 App 尚无 App 内删除账号按钮。请向 hello@pulseandpurpose.net 发送主题为“故事 App 账号删除”的邮件，说明对应账号（例如含国家或地区区号的手机号），以及希望删除整个账号和相关数据，还是仅提出特定数据请求。

我们会在处理前核验请求权限并说明范围。请勿发送密码或短信验证码。此流程由人工处理，不承诺即时删除或固定完成时限；如有必须保留或需要分别核实的共享信息，我们会说明。退出登录、离开家庭或清除设备本身，不会删除云端账号；已导出或被他人保存的副本无法撤回。仅存在本机的内容，请先导出所需记录，再通过 App 或设备、浏览器的存储管理功能移除。

有人未经同意加入或分享了我的信息

请邮件提供相关故事链接或足以定位内容的信息，并简要说明问题。不要发送超出处理所需的个人信息。没有账号也可以联系我们；版权和滥用问题同样可通过此邮箱报告。
